Mindstorms in Natural Language-Based Societies of Mind

Natural language-based societies of mind for open-ended collective intelligence.

Venue: NeurIPS Ro-FoMo 2023 (Oral, Best Paper Award) Area: Multi-Agent Systems Type: Paper + Code + Poster

Summary

Mindstorms studies language-native societies of mind, where multiple specialized agents interact and coordinate to solve complex tasks. The project explores open-ended collaboration dynamics and emergent collective intelligence.
